Why we built this

Grandma drove two hours. The field had changed. She missed the whole game.

It happens in every family. The schedule shifts, a game moves across town, a tournament gets added at the last minute — and the people who would give anything to be there are always the last to find out.

Every other app is built for team managers and busy parents. Nobody built one for the grandparent. So we did. I'll Be There puts the games, the times, and the directions right in the hands of everyone who loves a kid and wants to be present.

Because being there is the whole point.
