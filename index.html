Why we built this

Grandma has no idea what's going on this week. And she desperately wants to.

Across an extended family, the games and milestones live in a dozen places — a team app here, a group text there, a calendar nobody outside the household can see. So the people who love a child most are left guessing, or simply left out.

Every other app is built for the team manager. Nobody built one for the grandparent — or the aunt across the country, or the cousins who'd love to swing by. I'll Be There gathers every kid's games into one place and puts them in the hands of the whole family.

So when your granddaughter scores her first goal, you're either in the stands — or you're the first call she gets. That connection doesn't exist anywhere else today.

One family. One calendar. Every moment.

Built to keep the whole extended family connected — simple enough for anyone.

📅

Every kid, one calendar

All the games across your whole extended family in a single place. Catch your daughter's 10am and your nephew's noon game the same day. Games only — no practice clutter — plus family events like picnics and reunions.

☀️

Your morning game plan

Each morning, see every game happening that day across all the kids you love. Each one shows exactly how far away it is, and a single tap opens the map with turn-by-turn directions — so you can decide what to make it to and head straight there.

🙋

Say "I'll Be There"

Tap to tell the family you're coming. Parents know to expect you, and when several of you are going, it's easy to plan lunch together after the game.

👪

Every family, however it's shaped

Two parents, co-parents raising kids across two homes, blended families, grandparents, guardians, nannies, and caregivers — anyone who helps raise a child can take part and pitch in. Each person gets a role, and only ever sees what the parents allow.

🔒

You decide who sees what

Parents are always in control — down to each person. You choose who gets updates about each child: Grandma, yes. Your sister, no. Your aunt, yes. Nobody is added by default, and no one ever sees more than you decide. No awkward group texts, no hurt feelings — just the right people cheering on the right kid. Change it anytime.

🧡

Close, even from far away

Can't make it from a thousand miles out? Get the result the moment the game ends — so you can call to celebrate the win, or lift them up after a loss. A family touchpoint that didn't exist before.
