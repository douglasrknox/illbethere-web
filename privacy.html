Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 31, 2026

I'll Be There ("we," "us," or "our") operates the I'll Be There mobile application and the website at illbethereapp.com (together, the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. We built this app for families, and we take the privacy of those families — especially children — seriously.

Information We Collect

We collect only what we need to help your family stay connected:

  • Account information: your name and email address, used to create and secure your account.
  • Family and activity information: children's first names, group names, and activity details (such as game dates, times, and locations) that you choose to add.
  • Location information: a home-base location and, with your permission, your device location, used to show you which games are happening nearby. You control this and can turn it off at any time.
  • Usage information: basic technical data such as device type and app interactions, used to keep the Service working reliably.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide the core features of the Service — showing schedules, locations, and directions to your family.
  • To send you account-related and activity notifications you've asked for.
  • To secure accounts and prevent abuse.
  • To improve and maintain the Service.

SMS and Messaging

We do not sell or share your phone number or messaging information with third parties for their marketing purposes. If you opt in to receive text messages from us, message and data rates may apply, and you can opt out at any time by replying STOP. Consent to receive messages is not a condition of using the Service.

Children's Privacy

I'll Be There is designed to be used by adults on behalf of their families. Activity information about children is added and controlled by a managing parent or guardian. Children under 13 do not hold their own accounts. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only:

  • With other members of family groups you choose to join or create.
  • With trusted service providers who help us operate the Service (such as hosting and database providers), under confidentiality obligations.
  • When required by law or to protect the safety of our users.

Data Retention & Your Choices

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information at any time by contacting us. You can also disable location sharing and notifications from within the app.

Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections and secure authentication. No method of transmission is ever 100% secure, but we work hard to safeguard your family's data.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we'll revise the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app.

Contact Us
